How to Book Your Appointment

Booking takes less than a minute. Just follow these four easy steps:

  • Step 1 - Pick what you want done: Tick the nails, lashes, pedicure, facial, makeup, or piercing services you want, then tap Next.
  • Step 2 - Choose your day and time: Tap a date on the calendar (we are open Monday to Saturday, closed on Sundays) and pick a time that suits you.
  • Step 3 - Enter your name and phone number: Type your full name and Ghana phone/WhatsApp number so we know who is coming.
  • Step 4 - Send on WhatsApp: Check your summary and tap Confirm on WhatsApp. WhatsApp will open with your booking already typed out. Just press Send!

Prefer to chat or call directly? Message us on WhatsApp (050 274 3916) or call 050 274 3916 during opening hours.

In-Person Payment Only

  • Nothing is charged online when you submit a booking request.
  • Settle your bill in person at the studio after your treatment via Cash or Mobile Money (MoMo).
  • For custom nail art or Long Acrylics (starting from GH₵200), your technician confirms the exact price before starting.

Cancellation & Lateness Policy

At Glamorous Nails, we value every client's time and strive to provide the highest level of service in a relaxed and professional environment. To help us serve all clients efficiently, we kindly ask that you review our appointment policy.

Appointment Cancellations

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please provide at least 24 hours' notice. This allows us the opportunity to offer the appointment time to another client.

Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a cancellation fee or may require a deposit for future bookings.

Late Arrivals

We understand that delays can happen. If you are running late, please contact us as soon as possible.

  • Arrivals of up to 15 minutes late will be accommodated where scheduling permits.
  • Clients arriving more than 15 minutes late may need to have their service adjusted, shortened, or rescheduled to avoid disrupting other appointments.
  • In some cases, the appointment may be considered a missed appointment and cancelled.

No-Shows

Clients who miss an appointment without notice may be required to pay a booking deposit before scheduling future appointments.

Booking Deposits

Certain services, group bookings, and lengthy nail appointments may require a non-refundable booking deposit. Deposits will be applied toward the final service cost.

Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a clean, welcoming, and punctual experience for every client. Your cooperation helps us maintain the quality and professionalism that Glamorous Nails is known for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to pay a deposit online to book?
No. Payment is strictly in person at the studio after your appointment via cash or Mobile Money (MoMo).
What happens if I am stuck in Accra traffic?
Message us on WhatsApp (050 274 3916) as soon as possible so we can check if your technician can still fit your full treatment or move your time.
Can I book nails, lashes, and pedicure together?
Yes. Select multiple services in Step 1 above and our time-slot picker automatically checks total duration against studio closing hours.
Where exactly are you located?
We are at Haatso Supermarket Junction, Accra. You can message us on WhatsApp for a quick landmark pin if needed.
Is there an age requirement for piercings?
Yes. Piercing clients must be 18+ or accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. All needles and jewellery are sterilised.
