Meet the Founder

Tsodzinee Adra founded Glamorous Nails after years of professional training, hands-on experience, and a passion for exceptional nail care. Starting her journey under experienced industry professionals, she dedicated herself to mastering the art and science of healthy, beautiful nails before opening her own studio at Haatso Supermarket Junction.

Today, she is known for her attention to detail, commitment to hygiene, and high-quality workmanship. Her dedication has earned her a loyal client base and allowed her to mentor aspiring nail technicians, many of whom have successfully completed their training and launched careers of their own.

At Glamorous Nails, every client receives personalised care in a clean, welcoming environment where nail health and lasting beauty always come first.
