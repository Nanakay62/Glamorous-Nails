What We Teach

Everything you need to start.

Train in a single specialty or combine courses to become an all-round nail and beauty technician.

Nail Fixing & Acrylics

Tip sizing, short, medium and long acrylic sculpting, stick-on fixing, shaping, e-file control, refills, and clean cuticle work.

Gel Polish & Nail Art

Natural nail prep, base/top application, French tips, chrome, ombre, and neat finishing without lifting or flooding.

Manicure & Pedicure

Warm soak, cuticle care, callus smoothing, exfoliating scrub, relaxing massage, tool sterilisation, and clean polish finish.

Eyelash Fixing

Classic, hybrid, volume, and strip lash application, eye-shape styling, safe adhesive handling, isolation, and gentle removal.

Microblading & Brows

Facial symmetry, brow mapping, shaping, fine hair-stroke microblading technique, pigment selection, hygiene, and aftercare.

Makeup Artistry

Skin preparation, undertone and foundation matching, soft glam, bridal/event looks, brow definition, and strip-lash pairing.

Apprenticeship Agreement: Items to Provide & Studio Rules

1. Items to be provided by the apprentice:

  • Three (3) sets of towels
  • Three (3) pairs of scissors
  • Two (2) trimmers
  • One (1) cotton roll
  • Three (3) nail files
  • Three (3) buffers
  • One (1) pack of normal nails
  • One (1) pack of French tips
  • One (1) crate of malt

2. Rules & Work Hours:

  • Work Hours: Reporting time is 8:00 AM; closing time is 7:00 PM or later as required (Mon–Sat, flexible to your selected training days).
  • Salon Cleanliness: Apprentices are responsible for keeping the salon clean at all times.
  • Conduct & Visitors: Misconduct, stealing, quarrelling, misuse of materials, and inviting friends during work hours are strictly prohibited.
  • Attendance & Duration: Lateness or unexcused absence attracts penalties. Training is expected to be completed within 1 to 2 years.
